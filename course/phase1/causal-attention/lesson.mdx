## Intuition

Self-attention lets each position build a content-dependent weighted combination of representations. A decoder adds the causal invariant: position `i` may depend on tokens at positions `j <= i`, but never on future positions `j > i`.

> **Causal invariant:** if two sequences are identical through position `i`, changing only future positions must not change outputs through `i` within numerical tolerance.

## Formalism

For normalized hidden state `X`:

$$
Q=XW_Q,\qquad K=XW_K,\qquad V=XW_V
$$

The causal attention operator is:

$$
A=\operatorname{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}+M\right)
$$

Future positions in `M` are negative infinity. The Phase 1 implementation evaluates softmax in FP32 before converting probabilities back to the working dtype.

## Tensor shapes

The canonical 100M configuration uses twelve query heads and four key/value heads:

- `Q`: `[B, 12, S, 64]`
- `K`: `[B, 4, S, 64]`
- `V`: `[B, 4, S, 64]`
- grouped `Q`: `[B, 4, 3, S, 64]`
- explicit score tensor: `[B, 4, 3, S, S]`

The final dimension is the 64-dimensional head space; the two `S` dimensions in the score tensor create the visible quadratic memory component that this phase deliberately measures.

## Causal masking

A triangular mask is not accepted merely because it looks correct. The required behavioral test constructs two inputs that match through position `i` and differ after it; outputs through `i` must remain equal within tolerance.

Masking is applied before softmax. Applying a mask after softmax violates the causal probability distribution.

## GQA mechanics

Grouped-query attention exposes a group dimension in Q:

$$
Q\rightarrow[B,H_{kv},G,S,d_h]
$$

with `Hkv = 4` and `G = 3`. K and V conceptually have a singleton group dimension and broadcast during the score/value matmuls. This is mathematically equivalent to explicit repetition without making persistent twelve-head K/V storage part of the implementation.

## Failure modes

Common failures include:

- masking after softmax;
- applying RoPE to V later in the integrated model;
- permanently repeating K/V to twelve heads;
- evaluating softmax entirely in FP16;
- reshaping the query/KV group order incorrectly.
